## 1.0.0 (2026-11-12)

First release, with six skills:

- `fetch-rules`: polite fetching from other people's servers.
- `code-of-ethics`: the ACM Code of Ethics applied to computing work.
- `no-generated-art`: human-made names, icons and art only.
- `wrap-up-timer`: wrap-up nudges on a real clock.
- `sarc-check`: who to contact before sending archival astrometry to the MPC.
- `no-ai-slop`: by Peter Yang, included unchanged under MIT.
